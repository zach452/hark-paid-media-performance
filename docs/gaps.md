## Decisions (Oct 19, artifact build)

- **#1 Google Search installs:** count all Google Ads conversions as installs, as the original parser did when an export had no "Installs" column (Installs ?? Conversions). This is your decision. Search CPA is therefore on all conversions.
- **#3 Meta 3-second plays:** Shadow's daily account total is split across ads in proportion to 2 × 25% views − 50% views, rounded so the per-ad values add back to the daily total. Totals over all of Meta are exact; per-ad and per-objective hook rate, hold rate and cost per 3-second play are estimates, marked *. Backtest against the Ads Manager 3-second plays for Oct 14–18:
  - Per ad: 7.6% spend-weighted error, 9.5% median.
  - Per objective: Traffic hook 7.5% vs 7.0% true, Engagement hook 62.0% vs 68.8% true.
- **#4, #9, #10:** Meta shares, saves and comments, and TikTok multi-ad reach, frequency and custom engagements show "—", not 0. They can't be derived from what Shadow stores.
